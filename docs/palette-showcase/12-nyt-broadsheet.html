In Sweeping Audit, Repository Found Healthy as Design Debate Intensifies

A review of 20 skills reveals a codebase in robust condition, though engineers flag two structural debts they say cry out for retirement before the next quarter.

The SKILLS repository, a growing collection of agent skills for the Crush platform, has reached a level of maturity unusual for a project of its type, auditors said Thursday.

Twenty skills are now under active maintenance, with seventeen passing every quality gate the audit could throw at them. But beneath the surface, two structural debts were identified.

Indigo on slate is the most copied AI palette in production today. It has to go.
Key Findings
Critical

Palette is unoriginal

The current colours are Tailwind's stock defaults — indigo on slate-900.

Important

No signature hue

The kit lacks a memorable accent colour to distinguish its reports.

Commendable

Components travel well

Stat cards, issue cards, and callouts are reusable across all skills.

// propagate the chosen palette to all 11 consumers
const palette = "nyt-broadsheet";
sync(11).skills.forEach(s => s.recolor(palette));
