The SKILLS repository, home to a growing collection of agent skills for the Crush platform, has reached a position of unusual maturity for a project of its kind. Twenty skills are now under active maintenance, with seventeen passing all quality gates.

Yet beneath the surface, our auditors identified two structural debts that warrant immediate attention. The visual design system, they note, leans heavily on recognisable defaults.

Indigo on slate is the most recognisable AI palette in production. It must go.
