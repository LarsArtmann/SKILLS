Repository found healthy as palette debate rages on

A sweeping audit of 20 skills and 11 vendored copies reveals a codebase in robust shape, though two structural debts cry out for retirement before the next quarter.

The SKILLS repository, home to a growing collection of agent skills for the Crush platform, has reached a position of unusual maturity for a project of its kind. Twenty skills are now under active maintenance, with seventeen passing all quality gates.

Yet beneath the surface, our auditors identified two structural debts that warrant immediate attention. The visual design system, they note, leans heavily on recognisable defaults that undermine the project's identity.

The component vocabulary — stat cards, issue cards, callouts — is structurally sound and travels well across skills. The bones are good. The skin needs work.

Indigo on slate is the most recognisable AI palette in production today. It has to go.
Key Findings
Critical

Palette is unoriginal

The current colours are Tailwind's stock defaults — indigo on slate-900. The most copied AI palette in production.

Important

No signature hue

The kit lacks a memorable accent colour. Reports are interchangeable with any AI-generated export.

Commendable

Components travel well

Stat cards, issue cards, and callouts are reusable across all skills. The structure is sound.
